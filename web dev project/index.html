About Our School

Greenwood High School was founded in 1995 with a mission to deliver well-rounded, value-based education. Over the past three decades, we have nurtured thousands of students who have gone on to excel in academics, leadership, and public service.

Our experienced faculty members employ progressive teaching methodologies, balancing rigorous academic standards with co-curricular growth, sportsmanship, and civic responsibility.

Our Core Values: Integrity • Discipline • Academic Rigor • Compassion • Continuous Learning

Academic Programs

Primary School (Classes 1 - 5)

Fosters foundational literacy, numeracy, creative expression, and curiosity through collaborative, activity-based learning.

Middle School (Classes 6 - 8)

Broadens knowledge across Science, Mathematics, Social Studies, and Languages while developing analytical and problem-solving skills.

Secondary School (Classes 9 - 10)

Prepares students for board examinations with intensive conceptual coaching, regular assessments, and dedicated academic counseling.
